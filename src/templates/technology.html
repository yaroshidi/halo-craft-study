01 · Capture Sleeve™

Imitation with consent

Operators wear the Capture Sleeve™ in consented training homes. It records the shape of a chore — reach, place, rinse, fold — so Halo can rehearse the motion language before it ever rolls into a founding household.

Your Tuesday night stays yours. Training data is not harvested from beta kitchens by default.
