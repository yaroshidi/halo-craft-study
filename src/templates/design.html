Design

Built for real rooms

Soft shell, wipeable seams, colors that sit next to oak and plaster — not a showroom gloss.

01 · Form

Quiet presence

Rounded volume, no sharp corners at kid height, a single calm LED that dims when the room is dark.

Halo should read as furniture that moves — not a gadget pile.

02 · Materials

Easy to clean

Matte polymer shell, textile sleeve on the tray, sealed seams you can reach with a cloth. No chrome fingerprints.

Shell

Impact-soft polymer with a wipeable finish.

Tray

Removable liner for crumbs and condensation.

Wheels

Soft compound for wood and tile — no black streaks.

Seams

Fewer gaps. The ones that remain open for a cloth.

03 · Colorways

Four quiet finishes

Named for rooms, not marketing seasons.

Plaster
Warm light grey

Sage
Soft green

Clay
Muted sand

Ink
Deep green-black

04 · Safety

Stops when people are close

Bumpers, proximity hold, and a clear pause affordance on the top shell. Halo yields to humans — always.

05 · Personalization

Your places, remembered

Show Halo where mugs live once. Prefer quiet hours. Hide the LED. None of that needs a cloud account for basic use.
